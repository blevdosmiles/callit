1. The records

  • Pikachu Illustrator (1998): given to the winners of an illustration contest in Japan, printed in very few copies. A PSA 10 copy holds the world record, with a sale of more than 5 million dollars (over 4 million euros).
  • First Edition Charizard from the Base Set: the most famous card in the game. In PSA 10 it has passed several hundred thousand dollars.
  • Tournament and trophy cards: given to winners of official competitions, in tiny numbers.
  • Rare promos: contests, events, Japanese magazines from the 1990s.

These prices are for graded copies in perfect condition. The same card damaged is worth far less.

2. What they have in common

  • Very few copies, often never sold in shops.
  • Perfect condition, certified by a grading company.
  • A Pokémon or a story people dream about: Pikachu, Charizard, the early days of the game.
  • Worldwide demand from collectors.

3. What about recent sets?

The most expensive cards in recent sets are usually the top rarities (special illustrations, gold cards) of the most popular Pokémon. Their price moves a lot in the first weeks after release, then settles.

Frequently asked questions

Are my 1999 cards worth millions?

Almost never. The records are for nearly impossible-to-find cards in perfect condition. But some holo cards from that era, well kept, are worth from a few dozen to several hundred euros.

Should I buy cards as an investment?

Nobody can guarantee that a card will go up, and prices can fall. Buy what you like first.
