1. The 30th anniversary sets

  • 30th Celebration: the main set, with the full card list.
  • 30th Classic Collection: classic cards reprinted for the occasion.

There are many products (collections, booster boxes, binders) and some sold out fast: always check what a product contains before paying above retail price.

2. Which cards are valuable?

  • The set's top rarities (special illustrations, gold cards).
  • Cards of the most popular Pokémon: Pikachu, Charizard, Eevee, Mew, Mewtwo…
  • The Japanese versions, often released first and closely followed.
  • Cards in perfect condition, which can be graded.

Commons and uncommons are worth little, even for an anniversary: they are printed in huge numbers.

3. Prices move fast

After a much-awaited release, prices are often high in the first days, then fall as packs reach every shop, and some cards climb back later. Look at the curve over several weeks before buying or selling.

4. Watch out for fakes

Popular releases attract fake cards and fake sealed products. Buy from well-known shops and check expensive cards: see how to spot fake cards.

Frequently asked questions

Should I keep 30th anniversary products sealed?

It's a bet: some sealed products gain value, others don't. Open what you want to open; only keep sealed what you can afford never to touch.
