1. Reading a One Piece card

  • The code, bottom right (for example OP05-119): the set, then the number. Main sets are called OP-01, OP-02, etc.; there are also starter decks (ST) and extra boosters (EB).
  • The rarity, next to the code: C (common), UC (uncommon), R (rare), SR (super rare), SEC (secret), L (Leader).
  • The language: Japanese, English and other languages depending on the region.

2. The cards worth a lot

  • Alternate arts (parallels): same card, different artwork, rarer.
  • «Manga» cards, illustrated with panels from the manga: among the most sought-after.
  • SP cards and special cards inserted in some sets.
  • SECs and alternate-art Leaders.
  • Popular characters: Luffy, Shanks, Zoro, Nami, Yamato…

3. What drives prices

The One Piece Card Game is played a lot in tournaments: a card that's strong in current decks can rise fast, then drop when the game changes. Collector cards (manga, alternate arts) depend more on the character's popularity and the artwork.

Frequently asked questions

Japanese or English?

Japanese cards come out first and are highly sought by collectors; English cards are often more expensive at the same rarity. Always compare the same language.

Are DON!! cards worth anything?

Regular DON!! cards are worth little, but some special or promotional DON!! are sought-after.
