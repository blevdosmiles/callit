Grading your Pokémon cards

Updated 27 November 2026

Grading a card means sending it to a company that checks it is authentic, gives it a grade out of 10 and seals it in a slab. Here is when it is worth it, and how to go about it.

1. What is grading for?

  • Proving the card is authentic: reassuring for a buyer.
  • Locking in its condition with a recognised grade from 1 to 10.
  • Protecting it in a sealed slab.
  • Raising its value: a card graded 10 can be worth several times the same raw card. A middling grade, however, may not even cover the cost of grading.

2. Which cards to send?

Grading has a cost (service, shipping, insurance) and takes time. It is worth it for:

  • cards that already have good value;
  • cards in excellent condition that could reach a 9 or 10;
  • cards you want to keep for a long time or sell to a demanding buyer.

For a common or worn card, it usually does not pay off.

3. What graders look at

  • Centring: the borders must be nearly even, front and back. It is the most common reason for a lower grade than expected.
  • Corners: sharp, with no white.
  • Edges: no marks, especially on the back.
  • Surface: no scratches, marks or print defects.

To find out which grade your card can aim for, read our guide Estimating your card's PSA grade.

Before sending, measure your card's centring with the "Worth grading?" tool in CallIt!: one photo of the front and back is enough.

4. Which company?

  • PSA: the best known in the world, widely recognised on resale, especially internationally. (80-90 working days)
  • PCA: a French company, widely used in France. (about 6 months)
  • Collect Aura: a French company, with one price for every card. (40 working days)
  • CCC: a European company. (10 working days)
  • BGS (Beckett): gives subgrades (centring, corners, edges, surface). Its top grade needs near-perfect centring. (75 working days or more)
  • CGC: a major international company. (about 50 working days)
  • Other companies depending on your country: GRAAD (Italy, 15 working days), AP Grading (Germany, about 20), AOG (Germany, about 40), ACE Grading (UK, 5).

Turnaround stated for the basic tier, in November 2026: it changes often, check each company's website.

The right choice depends on your country, budget, turnaround and where you want to resell. Look at which grades sell best for your card.

5. Preparing your submission

  1. Gently clean the card with a dry microfibre cloth, without rubbing hard.
  2. Put it in a new sleeve, then a semi-rigid holder or toploader, as the company asks.
  3. Fill in the online form: card, declared value, service level.
  4. Pack it securely between two rigid pieces of cardboard and send it tracked and insured.

Some collectors go through a shop or a group submission service: simpler, but compare the fees.

Frequently asked questions

How much does grading cost?

It depends on the company, the card's declared value and the turnaround you pick. Always check current prices on the official website, and add return shipping.

Can I challenge a grade?

Some companies offer a paid review. Otherwise you can crack the slab and resubmit, at the risk of the same or a lower grade.
