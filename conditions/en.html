What you buy

CallIt! Pro is a subscription that gives unlimited access to card condition checks (centering, visible defects, estimated grade and a picture to share) and to AI card readings in Fast mode.

Without Pro, each account gets 5 free condition checks a week (the counter resets every Monday) and 30 free AI readings a month. Everything else in CallIt! (prices, collection, wishlist, scan…) stays free.

You need a (free) CallIt! account to subscribe. Pro is tied to that account and works on all your signed-in devices.

Price

Two plans, VAT included: €5 a month, or €50 a year (the price of 10 months). The exact price is shown on the payment page before you confirm. There are no other fees.

If the price ever changes, we tell you by email at least 30 days before. You can cancel before the new price applies.

Payment

Payment goes through Stripe, a licensed payment provider. You enter your card (or another method offered) at Stripe: CallIt! never sees or keeps your bank details.

The first period (a month or a year) is paid when you subscribe, then each period on the same date until you cancel. For the yearly plan, we email you at least 7 days before each renewal. You get a receipt by email for each payment.

If a payment fails, Stripe retries for a few days. Without payment, your account simply goes back to the free version.

Duration and cancellation

The subscription has no commitment. You can cancel at any time with the « Manage my subscription » button in the app, or by writing to us.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid (the month or the year): you keep Pro until then, and nothing more is charged. The current period is not refunded, except on withdrawal (below).

Right of withdrawal (14 days)

You have 14 days from subscribing to withdraw, without giving a reason.

At checkout you ask for Pro to start right away (checkbox). If you withdraw within those 14 days, we refund the days left: you only pay for the days Pro was already running (for example, on the monthly plan, after 10 days out of 30, you get back about €3.30).

To withdraw: write a clear sentence to request@callitcards.com (for example: « I withdraw from my CallIt! Pro subscription », with your account's email address), or use the form below. We refund you within 14 days, with the same payment method, at no cost.

What a check is worth

A check is an automatic estimate made from your photos and depends on their quality (light, sharpness, framing). It is not an official grade: PSA, BGS, CGC and other grading companies are independent from CallIt! and set their grades on their own.

CallIt! therefore guarantees neither a grade nor a resale price. Use your own judgement before sending a card for grading or buying it.
